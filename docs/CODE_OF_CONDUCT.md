# Code of conduct

[Documentation index](README.md)

## Our commitment

Guardrail welcomes contributors and users of different backgrounds, experience
levels, identities, and perspectives. Everyone participating in project spaces
is expected to help create a respectful, constructive environment.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate, patient, and professional.
- Discuss code and ideas without attacking the people behind them.
- Give specific, actionable feedback and accept good-faith correction.
- Respect different experiences, accessibility needs, and communication styles.
- Respect privacy and other people's time and boundaries.
- Help newcomers understand the project and its contribution process.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, intimidation, stalking, or discriminatory abuse.
- Sexual harassment or unwanted sexual attention.
- Personal insults, deliberate humiliation, or repeated hostile disruption.
- Publishing private information or communications without permission.
- Retaliation against someone for reporting a concern or participating in a review.
- Ignoring reasonable moderation instructions or repeatedly crossing stated boundaries.

Good-faith technical disagreement is welcome. It must remain respectful.

## Scope

This policy applies to repository issues, pull requests, reviews, project
discussions, and other spaces maintained for Guardrail. It also applies when
someone explicitly represents the project in another space.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately to
[moeidheidari73@gmail.com](mailto:moeidheidari73@gmail.com).
Include relevant links, dates, a description of what happened, and any safety or
privacy concerns. Share only information needed to understand the report.

Do not publish sensitive reports in a public issue. If there is immediate danger,
contact appropriate local emergency or safety resources rather than waiting for
project moderation.

## Handling reports

The maintainer will review reports, seek additional context when appropriate, and
choose a response proportionate to the behavior and its impact. Reports will be
handled as privately as practical; absolute confidentiality cannot be guaranteed
when investigation or protective action requires limited disclosure.

Possible responses include a request to stop or correct behavior, content removal,
a warning, temporary participation restrictions, or a permanent ban. Severe
incidents may warrant immediate restrictions without an earlier warning.

Moderation decisions should be fair, consistent, and focused on participant safety.
Requests for reconsideration may be sent to the same contact with additional
context. Retaliation is itself a violation of this policy.
